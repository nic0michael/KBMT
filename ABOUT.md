# KBmate

## Your Knowledge. Organised. Accessible. Intelligent.

### 1. What Is a Knowledge Base?

KBmate is a complete, open-source Knowledge Base application that allows organisations to create, manage, organise and share information from one central location. It provides the platform to build and maintain your organisation's knowledge repository.

### 2. What Does KBmate Give You?

- **Free and Commercial Content:** Access professionally developed Knowledge Bases supplied in HTML, Markdown and Microsoft Word formats.
- **Easy Import:** Each Knowledge Base is packaged as a ZIP file for straightforward uploading and importing.
- **Versioned Knowledge Bases:** Knowledge Bases are versioned, making it easier to manage and distribute content updates.
- **Create Your Own Content:** Develop your own articles and Knowledge Bases, manage them and export your content.
- **Source Code Included:** Customise and extend the application to meet your organisation's specific requirements.
- **Database Backup and Restore:** Your DevOps team receives shell scripts to back up and restore the PostgreSQL database.
- **Role-Based Access:** Control who can administer, create, approve, publish and read information.
- **Self-Hosted:** Run the application using Docker and virtual machines on your own infrastructure.
